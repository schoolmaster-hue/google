📧 Messages & Updates

📅 October 8, 2026 - LATEST

🦅 AIR COMBAT RULES, OVERTIME TIEBREAKER, FIVE SERVER & RESPONSIVE INVENTORY UPDATE!

• Ground Melee vs Air Combat Rules: Ground melee troops can no longer attack flying air units (Minions, Baby Dragon, Minion Horde, Inferno Dragon, Mega Minion) and will pathfind to ground units, towers, or buildings. Ranged units, spells, towers, and flying units continue targeting air units normally.
• Melee Splash & Slam Protection: Melee splash damage (e.g. Valkyrie) and Mega Knight crater slams strictly ignore airborne targets.
• Air Elevation & Dynamic Shadows: Flying air units now render a distinct ground shadow directly beneath them and hover with a smooth animated aerial bob.
• River Flight Pathing: Air units now soar directly over the river in a straight line without bottlenecking at bridges.
• Sudden Death in Overtime: In Overtime, taking a single tower ends the match instantly with a sudden-death victory.
• Tiebreaker Tower HP Drain: If Overtime expires in a tie, every active tower steadily counts down HP until the lowest-health tower falls, awarding an immediate victory.
• Hand UI Selection Flicker Fix: Card selection in battle is now completely smooth with no DOM recreation, eliminating card flashes or reloads.
• Responsive Inventory & EVO Slots: Deck and inventory card slots now use responsive aspect ratios and scaling, keeping EVO slots and cards perfectly proportioned on smaller and mobile screens.
• Five Server Local Testing: The Debug Menu button is fully accessible when running on Five Server (127.0.0.1 / localhost) and file protocol, while staying hidden on remote GitHub Pages.
• Anti-Copy & Code Protection: Disabled text selection, right-click context menu, DevTools keyboard shortcuts, and drag-and-drop copying.
• Chest Rewards & Unboxing Mobile Scaling: Chest reward popup and opening animation are now fully responsive on smaller and mobile screens with scrollable reward items and guaranteed visible, accessible Continue ("Awesome!") buttons.
• Legendary Wildcards Renaming: Corrected wildcard inventory label to "Legendary Wildcards".
• Shard Inventory UI Layout & Arena Unlock Gating: Cleaned up the Shard Inventory cards so they no longer stretch, overlap, or clip. The Shard Inventory panel is completely hidden until unlocking Evolution cards at Arena 2 (300+ Trophies).

📅 August 24, 2026

👑 FULLSCREEN CHEST UNBOXING, PASS ROYALE OVERHAUL & UNIVERSAL PREVIEWS!

• Fullscreen Chest Unboxing: Tapping a chest opens a thrilling fullscreen star upgrade sequence with direct stars underneath. Tap to upgrade up to 5 stars—no extra button needed! The final tap bursts open directly into your rewards!
• Clean Chest Inventory: Unopened chest slots now display clean emojis without pre-populated stars.
• Pass Royale UI Overhaul: Completely refreshed Season Pass with custom Tier badges, crown progress bar, and clickable reward previews with detailed lore & claim buttons.
• Universal Sandbox Previews: Inspect and test ANY card in the sandbox arena, even before unlocking it in Trophy Road.
• Interactive Emote Audio: Tap any locked or unlocked emote in your collection or daily shop to hear its custom voice sound effect.
• Strict Arena 2 Evolution Gating: Evolutions strictly remain locked until reaching Arena 2 (300 Trophies), with a clear milestone banner on Trophy Road.
• Max Arena Trophy Cap (2700 🏆): Wins at Arena 10 (Legend Summit) award full chests and Season Pass crowns while capping trophies at 2700; losses allow dropping down 30 trophies so you can climb back.
• Independent Tower Loadouts: Casual 1v1 and Trophy Road maintain separate tower loadouts so casual experimentation never disrupts your ranked ladder loadout.

📅 August 24, 2026

⚡ SKELETON GROUP, ZAPPIES & COLLECTION SORTING UPDATE!

• Skeleton Group: Spawns a massive 15-skeleton swarm in concentric circles for 3 Elixir (Arena 2 Epic)!
• Zappies: Deploys 3 miniature zap shockers shooting fast electric bolts with 0.5s stun on hit for 4 Elixir (Arena 7 Rare)!
• Collection Sort & Hide Locked: Filter and sort your card collection by Arena Unlock, Elixir cost, or Rarity, plus a quick toggle to hide all locked cards.
• EVO 3-Check Rule: Evolutions only activate when all 3 checks pass: EVOs unlocked in general, card EVO unlocked, and card placed in active EVO slots 1 or 2.
• King Tower Range Buff: King Tower reach increased to 225 so it can support defenses against units attacking Princess towers.
• Storm Wizard Range Fix: Dual-zap electric bolts now strictly stay within unit attack range.
• Localhost Debug Devtools: Enhanced testing tools exclusively accessible during local development sessions.

📅 August 24, 2026

😡 THE ANGER UPDATE & UNIQUE CARD IDENTITY OVERHAUL!

• Unique Card Names & Descriptions: Every card now features custom emoji-lore names (Blade Vanguard, Colossus Titan, Boomstick Belle, Arcane Pyromancer, Obsidian Juggernaut, Solar Beam Drake, Thunder Magus, Crater Titan, Tesla Overcharger, Timber Berserker, Aether Bowmaster, Rolling Trunk, and more), comprehensive card descriptions, and clear unit classifications (Ground Unit, Air Unit, Building, Spell)!
• Anger Puddle (Spell): Added the Anger spell card (Cost 2 / Epic). Casts a 5.0-second bubbling Anger puddle giving all friendly units +40% movement speed and attack speed with a glowing red fury aura & red sparkles.
• Timber Berserker Anger Drop: On death, Timber Berserker drops a 5.0-second Anger puddle at his location.
• 3-Second Lingering Fury: When units leave the Anger puddle, their Anger buff, glowing red fury aura, and red sparkles linger for 3.0 seconds after leaving. While inside, the Anger effect refreshes continuously.
• Solar Beam Drake (Inferno): Continuous fiery beam ramping over 3 stages (35 ➔ 180 ➔ 850 DPS).
• Casual Mode Towers: Towers automatically scale to Level 15 in Casual Mode with free tower selection!

⚡ NEW EVO: Goblin Gang Evolution!

• 6-Goblin Squad: Spawns 3 melee stab goblins in front + 3 ranged spear goblins in the back.
• Ghost Anchor Soul Link: Deceased goblins queue up and respawn one-by-one from the central ghost anchor every 3s as their respective type.
• Squad Wipeout: If all living goblins die simultaneously, the ghost anchor dissipates immediately.
